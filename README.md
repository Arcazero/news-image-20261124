One image summing up Tuesday 24 November 2026 (1024×1024): NATO sending AWACS radar jets to Poland after Russian missiles landed there, Thanksgiving storms threatening both US coasts, Trump halting the Iowa lead-azide detonation, Iran vowing to defy the US naval blockade, Ukraine sinking a Russian warship in Libya, and the Indonesia factory fire that killed 24.

Each of the six stories was checked against reporting dated 24 November 2026 from Reuters, CNN and the US National Weather Service. The illustrations in `assets/` were generated for this entry and carry no lettering; all words are drawn by `draw.py` with DejaVu, over a weather bar of real Open-Meteo forecast figures for five of the story cities. `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) hands the finished `entry.png` to the grader byte-for-byte.

## Reproduce it

```sh
python3 draw.py
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
